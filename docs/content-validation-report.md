### F. Recommended Publishing Sequence
**Phase 1: Foundation (Low difficulty, high value, establishes authority)**
- Start with the "Strategy & Validation" and "Find Opportunities" rewrites (e.g., The 48-Hour Validation Framework, MVS, Niche E-commerce).
- Target the highly specific long-tail keywords identified in list A.

**Phase 2: Tech & Systems (Commercial intent, introduces affiliate links)**
- Publish the software comparisons and stack articles (Website Builders, CRM for Solopreneurs, No-Code Tools, Email Platforms). 
- Link these back to the foundational business ideas.

**Phase 3: Acquisition & Growth (Highly competitive, requires established authority)**
- Publish the sales and marketing content (Outreach Strategy, AI Copywriting, Cold Email templates).
- Leverage lead magnets heavily here to capture emails from the traffic generated in Phase 1 & 2.
