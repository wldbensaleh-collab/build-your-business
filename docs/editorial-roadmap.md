# Editorial Roadmap

## 1. Blog Niche
Build Your Business is a practical entrepreneurship blog focused on helping beginners understand how to choose, validate, start, manage, and grow a real business.

## 2. Target Audience
Beginners and aspiring entrepreneurs looking to start, manage, and grow a practical business.

## 3. Core Positioning
Our core angle is the intersection of: **Business + Management + Information Systems + Technology**. The blog is NOT a generic "make money online" blog.

## 4. Five Official Pillars
1. **Business Fundamentals** (e.g., How businesses make money, Revenue, Profit, Costs, Business models, Break-even, B2B vs B2C, Startup vs business)
2. **Starting a Business** (e.g., Finding business ideas, Starting with no money, Choosing a business model, Finding problems to solve, Finding customers, Starting online, Starting while studying, Building a business from scratch)
3. **Making Money** (e.g., Making money from skills, Freelancing, Services, Pricing, First $100, Revenue streams, Recurring revenue, Online business models)
4. **Business Management & Systems** (e.g., Business processes, Systems, SOPs, Automation, ERP, CRM, KPIs, Dashboards, Technology for small businesses)
5. **Growing a Business** (e.g., Marketing, Sales, First 10 customers, Customer acquisition, Retention, Scaling, Hiring, Measuring growth)

## 5. SaaS + Service Business Special Focus
SaaS and Service Businesses should appear throughout the content strategy.
Important examples:
- Service Business vs SaaS
- SaaS business ideas
- SaaS validation
- Starting a service business
- Productized services
- SaaS pricing
- Service pricing
- SaaS vs agency
- Freelancing vs SaaS
- Scaling SaaS
- Scaling service businesses

## 8. Editorial Rules
- Prioritize practical and actionable business education.
- Avoid generic "get rich quick" content.
- Technology should support the business rather than become the main topic.
- SaaS and service businesses are strategic recurring topics.
- Articles should solve a clear reader problem.
- Articles should internally link to related content.
- Do not fabricate statistics or keyword metrics.
- Prefer original analysis, examples, frameworks, and practical explanations.
