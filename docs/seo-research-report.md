## 1. Service Business vs SaaS: Evaluating Your First Venture
- **PRIMARY KEYWORD:** service business vs saas
- **SEARCH INTENT:** Commercial Investigation
- **RELATED KEYWORDS:** saas vs agency, service vs software startup, start saas or agency, b2b saas vs service
- **SERP COMPETITION:** High intent, medium competition.
- **WHO CURRENTLY RANKS:** SaaS blogs, Medium articles, indie hacker forums (e.g., Reddit r/SaaS).
- **CONTENT GAP:** Most articles are heavily biased toward SaaS because SaaS founders write them. A balanced view for beginners focusing on cash flow (service) vs scale (SaaS) is missing.
- **COMMERCIAL / AFFILIATE POTENTIAL:** Affiliate (Website hosting, SaaS boilerplates).
- **RECOMMENDED ARTICLE ANGLE:** "Why Service Businesses Win for First-Time Founders (And When to Switch to SaaS)"
- **DECISION:** PUBLISH NOW
